## Summary
Across several architect/builder-mode sessions, `master_todo` was triaged by categorizing each entry as resolved (with evidence for safe removal), an actual issue (concrete next action), or an open question deferred to the user; this produced three recurring concrete action items — externalizing session-crawler regexes into config like mem-bank's `subscriptions.json`, adding a QC/review step to the Anki pipeline, and feeding accumulated feedback history into future card generation — plus open decisions about the feedback-marker system and manual small-bank bootstrapping that were revisited across multiple sessions without being finally settled. In parallel, the architect-mode README and `product-vision.md` were reframed to avoid duplication, the "open design questions" section was removed per the todo, and the grove-as-plugin metaphor was de-literalized (groves are generative specs relying on infrastructure, not infrastructure plugins themselves) — work that also touched a public-website rework (see [[world-adoption-outreach-and-disclosure]]). Later, the architect persona itself was refactored into a thin pattern: `context.md` shrunk to ~35 lines of role definition and operating principles with lazy-loaded pointers to `layers.md` and `structural-lessons.md`, a structure intended to support a future migration to an opencode agent definition (committed as `fd84ecc`). A separate process discovery emerged near the end of this arc: plans approved in plan-mode don't persist as repo files after `/clear` — they're buried in raw session transcripts — establishing the pattern that approved plans should be written to durable files under `modes/meta/architect/` (following existing examples like `dafne_plan.md`) rather than trusted to session memory.
